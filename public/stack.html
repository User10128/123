How to Play

Welcome to Stack Down. A minimalist solitaire variant.

  • The Deck: 40 cards total. Numbers 1 to 10 in two colors (Red and Blue), with two identical sets.
  • Tableau (Bottom 5 Stacks): Build stacks downwards (10 down to 1) in the same color (Red on Red, Blue on Blue).
  • Dragging: You can drag a single card, or a valid stack of cards.
  • Groups: Valid stacked sequences will visually merge with a bright outline when they are at the top of the stack.
  • Freecell (Top Middle): One blank section that can temporarily hold any single card.
  • Foundations (Top Right): Move a 1 to an empty foundation. Then build up (2, 3, 4... to 10) in the same color.
  • Stock (Top Left): Click to draw. Stock cannot be cycled through again.

Goal: Move all cards to the 4 finished foundation piles.
